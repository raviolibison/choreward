Privacy Policy for Choreward

Effective date: 2026-10-12

Choreward is a family app for managing chores and rewards. It is made by Ravioli Studios ("we", "us"), which is the data controller responsible for your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Who the app is for

Choreward is designed for families, and children, including children under 13, may use it. A parent or legal guardian creates the household. Children join it with a child invite code that only a parent in that household can share, and should only create an account and use the app with their parent's or guardian's permission. We recommend that a parent helps children under 13 set up their account.

Information we collect

  • Account information: the email address and display name of each parent and child who signs in. You can sign in with a password, which is handled by Firebase Authentication so we never see it, or with a Google account, in which case we receive your name and email address from Google. The display name can be a nickname.
  • Family content: your household's name, and the chores, points, rewards, and reward requests you create in the app.
  • Chore photos: when a child marks a chore as done, they take a photo with the device camera as proof. Photos are stored with your household and can only be seen by members of your household. We suggest avoiding faces and other identifying details in these photos.
  • Notification token: an identifier for your device, used only to send you notifications, for example when a chore is submitted or approved.
  • Purchase information: if your household buys Choreward Premium, the purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. We only receive whether your household has an active subscription.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, or the advertising ID. The app contains no ads, and we never sell your data or use it for marketing.

How we use your information

We use your information only to run the app: to sign you in, store and sync your family's chores, rewards, and photos across devices, send you notifications about your household, unlock Premium features for households that subscribe, and answer your support requests. We do not send marketing emails.

Children's privacy

We collect as little information about children as possible. A child's account needs an email address or Google account to sign in, a display name, and the chores, points, reward requests, and chore photos that come from using the app. A child's information is only visible to the members of the households they belong to. By setting up the household and letting a child join it, the parent or guardian consents to the processing described in this policy.

Parents and guardians can at any time review the information stored about their child, ask us to delete it, or refuse further collection by deleting the child's account (see Deleting your account and data). If we learn that we have collected information from a child without a parent's consent, we will delete it.

Legal basis for processing

Under the GDPR, we process personal data to provide the app you have signed up for (performance of a contract). We process children's data based on the consent of their parent or guardian. We keep purchase records where accounting and tax laws require it (legal obligation).

Third-party services

We use the following service providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf and do not use it for their own purposes.

  • Google Firebase, provided by Google: Firebase Authentication for signing in, Cloud Firestore for storing family data, Cloud Storage for Firebase for chore photos, Cloud Functions for Firebase for server-side processing, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • Google Play, provided by Google, for handling Premium subscription purchases and payments.
  • RevenueCat, for tracking whether a household has an active Premium subscription. RevenueCat receives purchase information linked to a random household identifier, not your name or email address. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

We do not share your data with anyone else, except where required by law.

International data transfers

Our service providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In that case, they rely on safeguards approved under the GDPR, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, to protect your data.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, your data is deleted right away. Deletion requests sent by email are completed within 30 days. Google Play and RevenueCat keep records of purchases for as long as accounting and tax laws require.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open Profile and tap Delete account. This deletes your account, your profile, and your points. In addition:

  • If you are the only parent in a household, the whole household is deleted for all of its members, including its chores, rewards, reward requests, and chore photos.
  • Otherwise, you are removed from the household, your chore photos are deleted, and your name is removed from its chores and reward requests.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store, under Payments & subscriptions.

If you cannot access the app, email support@ravioli-studios.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my Choreward account". We will confirm and delete your data within 30 days.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below. We will respond within one month.

If you believe we have mishandled your data, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Firebase, and access is restricted so that only members of your household can read your household's data and photos. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will let you know in the app.
