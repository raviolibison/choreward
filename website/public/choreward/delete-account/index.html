Delete your Choreward account

Choreward by Ravioli Studios

You can delete your Choreward account and the data connected to it at any time, either in the app or by email. Every parent and child has their own account, and each account is deleted separately.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Choreward and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap the Profile icon at the top of the screen.
  3. Scroll down, tap Delete account, and confirm.

Your data is deleted right away.

Request deletion by email

If you can't use the app, you don't need to have it installed:

  1. Send an email to support@ravioli-studios.com.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Choreward, so we can verify that the account is yours.
  3. Use the subject Delete my Choreward account.

We will confirm your request by email and delete your data within 30 days. A parent can also ask us to delete their child's account this way.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (email address, name, and login)
  • Your points and your device's notification token
  • If you are the only parent in a household: the whole household for all of its members, including all chores, rewards, reward requests, and chore photos
  • Otherwise: your membership in the household, your chore photos, and your name on its chores and reward requests

What may be kept

  • Records of Premium subscription purchases, which Google Play and RevenueCat keep for as long as accounting and tax laws require.
  • Any other information we are required to keep by law.

Premium subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store, under Payments & subscriptions, before you delete your account.

For more about how we handle data, see our privacy policy. Questions? Contact support@ravioli-studios.com.
